The project follows the MISRA rules for safety-criticla systems. MISRA comes from the automotive industry, and whilst it may seem confusing at first, these rules are
the closest to our product. It would, initially, make more sense to follow the NASA rules, but keep in mind we are talking about a C-UAS product vs a rocket which is
twice the size of the Bell Tower. 

MISRA Rules : https://meetingcpp.com/mcpp/slides/2025/Misra_talk_CppMeeting775955.pdf
